The CC license does not supersede the rights of KICE, EBS, EBSi, Megastudy, the Ministry of Education or others.

**Third-party materials:** Exam problems, exact answer explanations, PDFs, extracts, screenshots, publisher databases, and provider-specific statistics are **not sublicensed** by the maintainer. Each asset must store `rights_holder`, `source_url`, `license_url`, `permission_status`, and `redistribution_allowed`. Unknown permission means **do not distribute images or text**.

**Correct answer rate / cut scores:** Do not replace the origin of Megastudy aggregate sample statistics with KICE: the publisher and sampling methodology are different, even if the underlying questions originate with KICE. KICE-authored exam question copyright is separate from Megastudy's analytical data.

**KICE open-data notice:** one KICE national annual participation dataset is explicitly marked Korea Open Government License **Type 2**, requiring attribution and prohibiting commercial use. This applies only to that identified dataset, not every KICE work.

**KICE examination PDFs:** many include an explicit notice that KICE permission is required for reproduction and distribution. A source link and local-only clipping workflow are supplied instead of bundled copied questions.

**EBS/EBSi:** educational access alone does not grant a general reproduction or republication license. Before embedding or redistributing original text/crops, obtain and record permission. A user may not change EBS's terms by adding a noncommercial label.

**URLs/titles/factual bibliographic metadata:** these are tracked with provider attribution separately from source-work ownership. Do not suggest that hosting a derived catalog is endorsement by providers.
